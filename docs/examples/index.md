download them from the [`docs/examples`](https://github.com/iGW-TU-Dresden/PyTracerLab/tree/main/docs/examples)
folder on GitHub and run them yourself.

## Paper Examples

Step-by-step versions of the five examples in the PyTracerLab paper (Rudolph et al.,
*Environmental Modelling & Software*). The notebooks use the same data and settings as the
paper and show each step with its results. The figures and numbers of the paper are produced
by the scripts in the [`docs/examples/paper`](https://github.com/iGW-TU-Dresden/PyTracerLab/tree/main/docs/examples/paper)
folder, which also contains all data files.

```{toctree}
:maxdepth: 1
:caption: Paper Examples

paper/paper_example_1
paper/paper_example_2
paper/paper_example_3
paper/paper_example_4
paper/paper_example_5
```

The former examples 7 to 9 have been replaced by the paper examples:

| Former example | Now |
|---|---|
| 7: Analysis Involving Multiple Tracers (data of Example 1 of TracerLPM) | Paper Example 5 |
| 8: PyTracerLab Benchmarking (forward simulations and calibration compared with TracerLPM) | Paper Examples 1 and 2 |
| 9: Modelling with PyTracerLab in the Bayesian Statistical Framework (groundwater flow model) | Paper Example 3 |
